# 💧 JalDrishti AI  
### 🇮🇳 Water Vision — Intelligent Water Quality Platform for India

An AI-powered platform that helps people **analyze water quality, detect contamination risks, and get purifier recommendations** based on Indian drinking water standards (BIS).

---

## 🌍 The Problem
In many parts of India, people don’t know:
- If their water is safe to drink
- How to read water test reports
- Which water purifier they actually need
- Regional water contamination risks
- Whether water is safe for babies

**JalDrishti AI solves this with AI + water science + India-specific data.**

---

## 🚀 Features

### 💬 AI Chatbot
- Ask water-related questions
- Works in **English, Hindi, Hinglish**
- Understands **TDS, pH, Iron, Fluoride, Nitrate**
- Gives health advice and solutions

### 🎤 Voice Input
- Speak instead of typing
- Hindi + English supported
- Automatic speech-to-text

### 🧪 Water Quality Analyzer
Enter parameters like:
- pH
- TDS
- Turbidity
- Hardness
- Fluoride
- Nitrate
- Iron
- Chlorine

Get:
- 🟢 Safe
- 🟠 Moderate
- 🔴 Unsafe
- Overall water verdict
- Filter recommendation
- Estimated purifier price

### 🇮🇳 India Region-Aware AI
Knows regional water problems:
- Bihar — Arsenic
- Rajasthan — Fluoride
- Uttar Pradesh — High TDS
- Coastal areas — Salty water

### 🧃 Filter Advisor
Recommends:
- RO
- UV
- UF
- Iron removal filter
- Activated carbon filter
- With **Indian market price range**
